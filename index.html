FAQ

Straight answers.

What is ALKÉ Network?

A community you take part in, free, while it prepares a future post-quantum, proof-of-work network. ALKE is its coin. It isn't a trading platform.

Is joining free?

Yes. No payment is required and you don't need a crypto wallet to get started.

Are daily rewards actual coins?

Not yet. Participation rewards are provisional. They are not currently transferable or redeemable blockchain coins.

Why are rewards provisional?

The post-quantum network isn't live yet, and the rules for conversion, eligibility and distribution have to be finalised and published before any reward becomes an actual coin.

When can I claim?

When the post-quantum mainnet is running and an audited distribution system opens. There's no date yet. We'll tell you in the app and by email if you opt in.

What happens if mainnet is delayed?

Your reward history stays on your account and the roadmap shows the real status. We won't invent dates, and we'll say plainly when something slips.

Is ALKE listed on an exchange?

Claiming your rewards doesn't depend on any listing. A global exchange listing is a separate, future ambition, not a promise, and a coin existing on a network doesn't mean it has a price or a market.

Already hold ALKE from the existing network? See Existing Holder Information.

What determines my allocation?

Your verified participation, under conversion and eligibility rules that are published before claims open. Limits per member may apply. Until then, the app shows your eligible allocation as not set yet.

What happens if I lose access to my account?

You can recover it through your verified email, and Help can step in if that fails. We will never ask for your password, seed phrase or private keys.

Can I take part without inviting friends?

Yes. Inviting is optional. Daily drilling, games, learning and the community are open to everyone, and no one is ever asked to buy anything.

Is ALKÉ affiliated with Bitcoin?

No. We mention Bitcoin as an example of how a new digital network can start small and grow. ALKE won't necessarily follow its path, value or price.

What happens if a reward is disputed?

A person reviews it, and you can appeal. Your reward history shows the status of every entry, including any correction.

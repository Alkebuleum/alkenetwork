01 · Logos

Logo and icon

Use the lockup that matches the background. PNGs are transparent; SVGs are the vector masters for print and design work.

Do

  • Use the files exactly as supplied.
  • Pick the dark-bg or light-bg version to match the background.
  • Leave clear space of at least half the icon tile's height on every side.

Don't

  • Redraw, recolour, stretch, rotate or outline the logo.
  • Add shadows, glows or other effects.
  • Place it on busy images where it can't be read.

05 · Name & voice

How to write and talk about us

Confident, warm and welcoming, for people everywhere, including people new to digital currency. Plain words and short sentences. Explain; don't hype.

ALKÉ NetworkThe network. Always with the accent.
ALKEThe fuel of the network, as in “ALKE Rewards”.
AlkersOur members.
THE NEXT BILLION NETWORKBrand line.
Your place in tomorrow starts here.Tagline and closing line.
alke.network · app.alke.networkWebsite and app.

Say

  • Drilling for daily participation: “start your 24-hour drilling session”.
  • Alkers for members, and talk about the community and growing with the network.
  • ALKE Rewards for what members build up, always described as provisional.
  • Drilling is app participation: the phone does no computing.

Never say

  • “Mining”, “downline” or “team earnings”, or that people earn from other people.
  • ALKE amounts, prices, values or returns, or any promise of profit.
  • Anything about exchange listings. Claims open on the post-quantum mainnet.

When rewards come up, include

“Participation rewards are provisional. They are not currently transferable or redeemable blockchain coins.”

06 · Kedo

Kedo, the network's AI operator

Kedo builds, runs and grows the network, under human rules. Kedo's face is the logo: a 9 × 9 dot plate with calm eyes and a status light in the corner. The face shows how Kedo feels; the light shows how the network is.

Kedo has a palette of its own: dark plate, ivory dots, copper light. Show Kedo as drawn here, next to ALKÉ Network, never recoloured in ALKÉ Violet or Fuel Gold. More at kedo.im.

Hello“Welcome in, glad you’re here.”

Pick an expression below to see it here.

Twelve expressions, one calm voice

    Network states: severity lives in the light

      How Kedo is built

      • 9 × 9 dot grid
      • Eyes: 1 × 3 dots, 3 apart
      • Status light: top right
      • Plate corners: 22% of the plate
      • Under 32 px: 5 × 5 face, no light

      Colours

      Labels next to Kedo use IBM Plex Mono (free on Google Fonts).

      Do

      • Draw Kedo only as the dot plate: dots on the grid, nothing in between.
      • Use the 12 expressions as they are, and match the face to what Kedo is saying.
      • Show the network's state with the corner light: copper, amber, red or grey.
      • Write Kedo's lines calm, short and in the first person: “I’m on it.”

      Don't

      • Give Kedo a body or limbs, or redraw the face as a human or cartoon robot.
      • Show panic, anger or fear. The face stays calm; severity lives in the light.
      • Draw Halted without the red pause bars, or put a face on Offline.
      • Recolour Kedo, or put Kedo's face inside the ALKÉ logo.

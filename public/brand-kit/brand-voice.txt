ALKÉ NETWORK · BRAND VOICE FOR VIDEO SCRIPTS
Paste this at the top of a HeyGen script or Video Agent prompt.

WHO WE ARE
ALKÉ Network is a global community preparing for the next generation of
digital currency. ALKE is the fuel of the ALKÉ Network and the networks
connected to it. Members are called Alkers. Anyone can join for free and
take part every day in the ALKÉ app.

Brand line: THE NEXT BILLION NETWORK
Tagline: Your place in tomorrow starts here.
Website: alke.network   App: app.alke.network

HOW WE SOUND
- Confident, warm and welcoming, for people everywhere, including people new
  to digital currency.
- Plain words and short sentences. Explain; don't hype.
- Talk about the community, taking part every day, and growing with the network.

WORDS TO USE
- "Drilling" for daily participation ("start your 24-hour drilling session").
  Never say "mining".
- "Alkers" for members.
- "ALKE Rewards" for what members build up, always described as provisional.
- Spell the brand "ALKÉ Network" (with the accent). Write the fuel itself as
  "ALKE", as in "ALKE Rewards".

RULES EVERY VIDEO FOLLOWS
- Rewards are provisional. When rewards come up, say: "Participation rewards
  are provisional. They are not currently transferable or redeemable
  blockchain coins."
- Never give ALKE amounts, prices, values or returns, and never promise profit.
- Claims open on the post-quantum mainnet. Don't talk about exchange listings.
- Drilling is app participation: the phone does no computing.
- Invites are optional. Once the rig-level rules are approved, talk about
  "growing your rig". Never say people earn from other people, and never use
  words like "downline" or "team earnings".
- Don't use the line "You missed Bitcoin." in videos. It's for the website only.

CLOSING LINE
Your place in tomorrow starts here. Join free at alke.network.

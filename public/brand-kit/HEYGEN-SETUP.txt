ALKÉ NETWORK · BRAND KIT FOR HEYGEN
====================================

HeyGen calls a brand kit a "Brand System". It needs a paid HeyGen plan
(Creator, Pro, Business or Enterprise).


1. CREATE THE BRAND SYSTEM
   In HeyGen, click Brand in the left panel, then Brand Systems, then Add new.
   Pick any template, choose Customize, and replace everything in it with the
   files in this kit.
   Skip "Import from your website" until the new alke.network design is live:
   the import copies whatever is on the site today.


2. LOGOS TAB
   Primary logo:
     01-logos/alke-logo-horizontal-dark-bg.png

   Alternate logos:
     alke-logo-horizontal-light-bg.png   for light backgrounds
     alke-logo-stacked-dark-bg.png       square and vertical videos
     alke-logo-stacked-light-bg.png      square and vertical videos, light backgrounds
     alke-icon-tile.png                  corner logo
     alke-icon-white.png                 one-colour watermark on dark video
     alke-icon-violet.png                one-colour mark on light video

   The svg folder holds vector masters for designers. HeyGen takes PNG.


3. COLOURS (Brand Spec)
   Add these hex codes:
     Night        #0B0717   main background
     Panel        #160E2C   cards, second background
     Ivory        #F7F2E8   main text, light backgrounds
     Mist         #BDB6CC   secondary text
     ALKÉ Violet  #5E3AFF   primary brand colour
     Lilac        #A58BFF   accents on dark backgrounds
     Fuel Gold    #F5B84D   calls to action, key numbers
     Gold Ink     #1C1030   text on gold
   More detail in 03-colours/colours.txt.


4. FONTS (Brand Spec)
   Upload these from 02-fonts, then set:
     Display     Space Grotesk Bold       SpaceGrotesk-Bold.ttf
     Heading     Space Grotesk Bold       SpaceGrotesk-Bold.ttf
     Heading 2   Space Grotesk SemiBold   SpaceGrotesk-SemiBold.ttf
     Body        Inter Medium             Inter-Medium.ttf
     Caption     Inter SemiBold           Inter-SemiBold.ttf
   The other weights are there if you want them. JetBrains Mono is for small
   labels and numbers in text you add yourself.
   All three fonts are free to use under the SIL Open Font License; the
   licence files are in the same folder.


5. ASSETS TAB
   Upload:
     04-backgrounds   four backgrounds, each in 16:9 and 9:16
     05-end-cards     closing card in 16:9 and 9:16
     06-logo-intro    3.5-second logo intro in 16:9 and 9:16 (MP4, no sound)


6. BRAND GLOSSARY
   Brand > Brand Glossary. Add the terms in glossary.txt, and fill in how
   ALKÉ is said before you use it.
   HeyGen doesn't apply the glossary on its own: select it on every new video
   and every translation.


7. AVATARS TAB
   Add the avatar that will present ALKÉ videos. This kit doesn't include one.


8. EVERY TIME YOU MAKE A VIDEO
   - Choose the ALKÉ Network Brand System.
   - Select the glossary.
   - Paste brand-voice.txt at the top of your script or Video Agent prompt,
     so the script follows the brand rules.
   - End on the end card, or open with the logo intro.


ALKE-brand-sheet.png is a one-page summary of the logo, colours, fonts and voice.

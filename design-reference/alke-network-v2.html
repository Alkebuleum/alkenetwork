The problem

The next financial rails are being built now.

Tokenized assets and digital money can move across borders, but institutions still require trusted identity, privacy, regulatory control and accountable participation.

Without shared, purpose-built infrastructure, each institution is left to solve these requirements alone — or to adopt systems that were never designed with African regulatory and institutional realities in mind.

African institutions risk entering this new economy through infrastructure designed and governed elsewhere.

The solution

Shared infrastructure, governed by trusted institutions.

Alké Network enables participating institutions to experiment with digital assets, identity and settlement while sensitive data remains under institutional control.

During Phase 1, founding members can:

  • Simulate stablecoin and tokenized-asset transactions
  • Test privacy-preserving identity
  • Pilot cross-institution use cases
  • Prepare technical, risk and compliance teams
  • Help define governance and participation standards
